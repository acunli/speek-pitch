One loop: read it, argue it, keep it.

1Read it

A feed built from anything you’re into. Each story becomes a one-minute brief: what happened, the facts to keep, where people split.

2Argue it

Pick a side and say it out loud. Quibbit, our frog, takes the other side and counters with facts from what you read.

3Keep it

A report that scores how you argued, never what you think: the holes in your words, the facts you missed, the other side’s best case.

A debate, start to finish.

  1. 1Quibbit finds the question people actually disagree on.
  2. 2You drag yourself to a side.
  3. 3You argue it out loud. Speek transcribes it.
  4. 4Weak spots get underlined while you go.
  5. 5Quibbit counters with facts from what you read.
  6. 6Your report scores how you argued, not what you think.
