At every networking event and fireside chat, that question is where the real conversation starts.

The person who asked it wasn’t the best speaker in the room. They’d just read something, and brought it up.

Good conversation isn’t a talent. It’s having read something, and having said it out loud before.

Meet Speek, and the frog who disagrees with you.

Pick any story. Quibbit finds the question people actually split on, takes the side you didn’t, and argues it out loud for four rounds.

A debate, start to finish, in 25 seconds.

  1. 1Quibbit finds the question people actually disagree on.
  2. 2You drag yourself to a side.
  3. 3You argue it out loud. Speek transcribes it.
  4. 4Weak spots get underlined while you go.
  5. 5Quibbit counters with facts from what you read.
  6. 6Your report scores how you argued, not what you think.
